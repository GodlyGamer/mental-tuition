03B · Knowledge graph · neural view · the alternative to 03A

Your knowledge as a neural network, held in a glass cube.

The cube turns slowly, one revolution every 80 seconds. Topics are bright neurons, smaller neurons fill out each subject, and synapses link them, with nothing else in the cube. Drag to turn it any way you like (arrow keys work too) and double-click to reset. Hovering anywhere pauses the auto-turn; hovering a topic spins the same spectrum ring as a button and lights its connections. A Cloud / Neural switch lets you pick a view.
